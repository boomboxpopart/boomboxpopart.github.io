“I paint what I love: Israel!”

Sofia is a Tel Aviv painter who mixes 1990s MTV nostalgia, maximalist pop culture and lively figurative painting. The work runs from her city's falafel joints and second-hand shops to bright, stylised pop icons, painted flat and bold in acrylic and gouache.
