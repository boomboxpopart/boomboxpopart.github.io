Original paintings in acrylic and gouache on canvas. Street scenes from Tel Aviv and portraits of 1990s pop icons.

About

Sofia is a painter based in Tel Aviv. Her work draws on 1990s music television, maximalist pop culture and the everyday life of her city: falafel stands, barbers, nail salons and second-hand shops, painted in flat, saturated colour.

All works shown are one-off originals. For enquiries, write to @boomboxpopart on Instagram.
